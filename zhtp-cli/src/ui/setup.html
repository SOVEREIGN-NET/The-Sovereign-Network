Recovery Input
Seed Phrase

Paste the full phrase into any field or enter words one by one. Tab keeps natural browser navigation, and arrow keys or Enter advance through the grid. Accepts 20-word (legacy) or 24-word (BIP39) phrases — leave the remaining cells empty for a 20-word phrase.

Seed Hygiene

Observer Node Setup
Onboard Observer

This machine will run as an observer node on the Sovereign Network Your phone acts as the sole signer — no private key or seed phrase is ever entered on this desktop.

Step 1
Create Observer Identity

Generate a post-quantum Dilithium5+Kyber1024 keypair for this observer node. The keypair is stored locally in ~/.zhtp/keystore/observer/ — your phone never sees these keys

Phone is the Signer

The desktop holds only the observer node identity. Your Sovereign phone app signs the admission transaction — no private key material ever leaves your phone

What happens next

After generating the identity, a QR code will appear. Scan it with the Sovereign mobile app to authorize this node on-chain.

Step 2
Scan with Mobile App

Open the Sovereign mobile app, navigate to Observer Admission, and scan the QR code below. Your phone will prompt and sign.
